Family Moments hands you one thoughtful conversation starter at a time — swipe through a deck
of questions built to spark real conversation with the people you love. No accounts, no backend,
nothing leaving your device.

HOW IT WORKS
Swipe right for the next card, left for the previous one, up to focus a card full screen, or down
to hide a card you've already used (it stays out of rotation until you bring it back from
Settings). Filter the deck by category, or shuffle straight to a random question. A grid view lays
out every question in the current filter at a glance — tap any card to open it full screen.

FIVE CATEGORIES
🎉 Ice Breakers — light, easy openers for any gathering
📸 Memories — questions that bring shared moments back to life
❤️ Values — what matters most, and why
🔮 Future Dreams — hopes, plans, and what's next
🌻 Daily Life — the small everyday things worth talking about

BUILT FOR REAL CONVERSATIONS, NOT JUST A DEMO
• Hidden cards stay out of the deck until reset from Settings, so a family can work through the
  whole set without repeats.
• Question content ships in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, and German; the app's own
  interface is available in English and Portuguese, switchable anytime via Android's per-app
  language setting.
• Light, dark, and system theme.
• No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, and no data ever leaves your device — everything is stored
  locally.

WHY THIS EXISTS
The best family conversations rarely start on their own — someone has to ask the right question.
Family Moments keeps a whole deck of them in your pocket, ready whenever there's a car ride, a
dinner table, or a quiet evening to fill with something better than small talk.
