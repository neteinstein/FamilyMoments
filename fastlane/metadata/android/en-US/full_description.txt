Family Moments hands you one thoughtful question at a time — a deck of conversation starters designed to turn screen time, car rides, and dinner tables into real conversation.

No accounts. No sign-up. No ads. Just open the app and start talking.

HOW IT WORKS
Swipe through a deck of question cards, pick one at random, or browse the whole deck as a grid. Each card is a conversation starter — pick one, take turns answering, and see where it leads.

• Swipe left or right for the next or previous question
• Swipe up to focus a card full screen
• Swipe down to hide a card you've already used or don't want — it stays out of rotation until you reset it
• Shuffle to jump straight to a random question
• Switch to grid view to browse every question in the current filter at a glance

FIVE CATEGORIES
🎉 Ice Breakers — light, playful questions to get everyone talking
📸 Memories — revisit favorite moments and family stories
❤️ Values — what matters most to the people you love
🔮 Future Dreams — hopes, goals, and things to look forward to
🌻 Daily Life — everyday questions that open up real conversation

WHO IT'S FOR
Family dinners, road trips, sleepovers, first dates, team building, therapy and coaching sessions, classroom icebreakers, or just two people who want to talk about something other than the weather. Family Moments works for families, couples, friend groups, and anyone who wants a nudge past small talk.

PRIVACY BY DESIGN
Every question lives on your device. There's no account to create, no personal data collected, and nothing to sync. Your conversations stay between the people having them.

MULTI-LANGUAGE CONTENT
Question content is available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, and German.

Pick a card. Take turns. See where the conversation goes.
